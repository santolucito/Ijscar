# An Investigation into Whether Structured Prompting Affects the Similarity of AI-Generated Practice Questions to Teacher-Written Questions

Suyash Tripathy

**Affiliation:** Jordan High School

**IJSCAR** Vol. 3, Issue 3 (2026) &nbsp;·&nbsp; pp. –

**DOI:** [10.67149/yhjs2024.5/7aiw1q3e](https://doi.org/10.67149/yhjs2024.5/7aiw1q3e)

---

## Abstract

With the continued development of AI capabilities the effectiveness of structured prompting on current LLMs has come into question. This study investigated the extent to which structured-prompting instruction affected the similarity of AI-generated practice questions to teacher-written questions. Sixteen high school students created prompts before and after structured-prompting instruction across four subjects. Each prompt was tested across three LLMs with three generations per model and the resulting question sets were evaluated using cosine similarity semantic precision teacher coverage semantic F1 and diversity. Across all five metrics differences between the before- and after-instruction conditions were small and none were statistically significant. The findings suggest that under the conditions tested structured-prompting instruction did not consistently change the measured characteristics of practice questions generated by current LLMs. Future research should examine larger samples additional educational contexts and other prompting strategies. All raw data and analysis code needed to reproduce this study are available at [https://github.com/Redbaronlad/Prompt-ENG](https://github.com/Redbaronlad/Prompt-ENG) archived at commit 405f188.
